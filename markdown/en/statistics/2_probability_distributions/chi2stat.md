# chi2stat

Chi-square mean and variance

## 📝 Syntax

- [m, v] = chi2stat(nu)

## 📥 Input argument

- nu - positive scalar or array: degrees of freedom.

## 📤 Output argument

- m - array: mean values.
- v - array: variance values.

## 📄 Description


<b>chi2stat</b> returns the mean and variance of the chi-square distribution.

## 💡 Example



```matlab
[m, v] = chi2stat([1 2 3]);
```
