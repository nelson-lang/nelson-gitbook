# gamstat

Gamma mean and variance

## 📝 Syntax

- [m, v] = gamstat(a, b)

## 📥 Input argument

- a - positive scalar or array: shape parameter.
- b - positive scalar or array: scale parameter.

## 📤 Output argument

- m - array: mean values.
- v - array: variance values.

## 📄 Description


<b>gamstat</b> returns the mean and variance of the gamma distribution.

## 💡 Example



```matlab
[m, v] = gamstat([1 2 3], [4 5 6]);
```
