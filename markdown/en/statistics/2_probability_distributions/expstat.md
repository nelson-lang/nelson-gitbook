# expstat

Exponential mean and variance

## 📝 Syntax

- [m, v] = expstat(mu)

## 📥 Input argument

- mu - positive scalar or array: mean parameter.

## 📤 Output argument

- m - array: mean values.
- v - array: variance values.

## 📄 Description


<b>expstat</b> returns the mean and variance of the exponential distribution.

## 💡 Example



```matlab
[m, v] = expstat(3);
```
